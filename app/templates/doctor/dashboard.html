How patients move through the consultation process.

1. Registration: The desk officer registers the patient and opens an encounter.

2. Nursing assessment: The nurse records vital signs and nursing notes.

3. Doctor consultation: The doctor reviews the nursing assessment and documents the clinical encounter.

4. Submission: The doctor submits the consultation, closing the encounter.
